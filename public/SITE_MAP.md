## Entity Relationships

### Problems lead to Solutions

Example:
A business has messy books, disconnected systems, or payroll issues.

That leads to:
Accounting, Payroll, Reporting, CRM, Automation, or Integrations.

---

### Solutions reference Platforms

Example:
Accounting may reference QuickBooks Online, QuickBooks Enterprise, Xero, or Zoho Books.

---

### Platforms support Solutions

Example:
QuickBooks Online supports Accounting, Payroll, Payments, Reporting, and Franchise Operations.

---

### Who We Help filters the message

Example:
A franchise owner may need accounting, reporting, payroll, payments, and franchisor compliance.

---

### Resources support every phase

Guides, FAQs, videos, tools, and knowledge base articles should connect back to Solutions, Platforms, and BOSS phases.

---
